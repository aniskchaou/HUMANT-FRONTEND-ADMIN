About Humant

Built for modern HR teams

Humant was designed to eliminate the fragmentation that plagues HR departments — spreadsheets for attendance here, a separate tool for recruitment there, and email chains for approval. We brought everything together.

Built on Angular and Spring Boot, Humant is a fast, reliable, and customisable platform that adapts to your company's workflows rather than forcing you to adapt to ours.
